In scope

The agreement lists the Contact page among the five agreed designs, and swapping the form fields is part of designing it. This is covered work.

“Responsive designs for five (5) pages: Home, Shop, Product Detail, About, and Contact.”
Reply to the client

Yes, that’s covered — the Contact page is one of the five in our agreement, and the field changes are part of designing it. I’ll fold it into the current round and you’ll see it in the next hand-off.

Unclear

The agreement covers the five page designs but says nothing about a mobile app screen. It is neither included nor excluded — the terms are silent, which is a different answer from “no”.

Low confidence — the terms genuinely read more than one way here. Treat this as a starting point for the conversation, not a settled answer.
Reply to the client

Good question, and honestly our agreement doesn’t say either way — it lists five web pages and stops there. Before I start, can we agree whether the app screen sits inside this phase or becomes its own small piece of work?

Out of scope

The agreement covers three rounds of revisions on the agreed page set, and the client has already used all three. A fourth round is new work, even though revisions themselves are covered.

“Includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions across the five agreed page designs. Additional rounds are quoted separately.”

Happy to take another look at the pricing page. Our agreement covers three rounds of revisions and we wrapped the third last week, so this would be a fourth — I can quote it as a small add-on, or roll it into the next phase if you’d rather keep everything together.
